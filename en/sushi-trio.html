Sushi Trio

Welcome to the counter! Orders keep coming in: tap the food, serve it in threes and keep room in the holding area. A tasty, relaxing puzzle in the cozy atmosphere of a small Japanese restaurant.

How to play

  • Tap a food item to serve it: each order wants three matching items.
  • An item with no order waits in the holding area: keep some room!
  • Serve the front row to reveal the one behind.
  • Moving shelves, locks, falling dishes: new surprises as you progress, and boosters to get you out of a tight spot.

Sushi Trio is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases. No account needed: your progress stays on your device.
