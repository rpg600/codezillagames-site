Short description

A tasty, relaxing puzzle: tap the food, fill the orders and keep room in the holding area. Serve sushi, skewers and Japanese treats in orders of three!

Long description

Welcome to the Sushi Trio counter! Orders keep coming in, and it's up to you to serve the right food at the right time.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a food item to serve it.
• Each order wants three matching items.
• An item with no order waits in the holding area: keep some room!
• Serve the front row to reveal the one behind.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Short levels, easy to pick up, and more and more challenging.
• New surprises as you progress: moving shelves, locks, falling dishes and more.
• Boosters to get you out of a tight spot: hammer, wand, shuffle and more.
• Coins for every win and a winning streak that gives you boosters.
• Over 100 illustrated sushi, skewers and Japanese treats, in a cozy Japanese restaurant atmosphere.
• No account needed: your progress stays on your device.

Sushi Trio is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases. The ad-removal purchase removes banners and ads between levels; rewarded videos are still offered, only if you choose to watch them.
